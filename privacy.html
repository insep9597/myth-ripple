Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

In short: we do not run any servers and we never receive your data. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google, for the optional Play Games sign-in and for the ads (a banner at the bottom of the screen, and videos you choose to watch).

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Myth Ripple (the "Game"), made by ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the Game on Google Play.

1. Data stored on your phone

The Game saves your progress (solved levels, stars, scores, your Daily Puzzle, Speed Round and Endless results) and your settings in a file on your device. We cannot access this file. It stays on your device unless you sign in to Google Play Games (see section 2). Uninstalling the Game, or choosing Settings > Reset progress, deletes it.

2. Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the Game uses Google Play Games Services to:

  • show your Play Games display name and rank on the Game's leaderboards and submit your scores to them (total score, Daily Puzzle score and best Speed Round);
  • back up your progress file in your Google account (Play Games Saved Games), so it can be restored on another device.

This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play the whole Game without signing in.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, and rewarded video ads only when you ask for one, to receive a hint or to skip a level. There are no interstitial ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance information.

Google uses this data to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy and ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

4. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • We have no analytics or tracking of our own, and we do not sell or rent any data.
  • The Game has no chat and no user-generated content.

5. Data retention and deletion

Progress on your device is kept until you reset it or uninstall the Game. Reset progress also resets the cloud backup in Play Games Saved Games. Your Play Games profile, leaderboard entries and saved games are part of your Google account and can be managed or deleted through Google (Play Games app and your Google account settings). Data collected by AdMob is kept according to Google's policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Since we do not hold any of your personal data ourselves, requests about Play Games or ads data are best directed to Google. You can always contact us with questions (section 9).

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Game, please contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continuing to use the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
